# 社区行为准则 · Code of conduct

请尊重参与者，围绕具体问题讨论。欢迎不同经验与观点，不接受骚扰、歧视、威胁、公开私人信息或持续针对个人的攻击。

Issue 与 PR 应聚焦产品问题、证据和可执行改进。维护者可移除不当内容、结束偏离讨论或限制反复违反规则的参与者。遇到问题可通过 [@vista8](https://x.com/vista8) 请求私密沟通，不公开当事人的敏感信息。

English: be respectful and discuss concrete problems and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information and personal attacks are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close derailed discussions or restrict repeated violations. Contact [@vista8](https://x.com/vista8) to request a private discussion without publicly sharing sensitive information.
